🚀 Level 11 — Strong Foundation

Class 11 mein syllabus ka level badhta hai. Shuru se concepts samjho, notes banao aur har hafte revision karo.

📅 Weekly routine

Roz 2–3 focused sessions apne timetable ke hisaab se rakho. Har session: concept samjho → examples dekho → khud questions solve karo. Hafte ke aakhir mein weak topics revise karo.

🔬 Science stream

Physics: concepts aur numericals; Chemistry: concepts, reactions aur practice; Maths: regular problem-solving; Biology: diagrams, terms aur active recall. Apne chosen subjects ke hisaab se plan badlo.

📊 Commerce stream

Accountancy mein daily practice, Business Studies mein headings/examples, Economics mein concepts aur graphs. Har chapter ke short notes aur practice questions banao.

🌍 Humanities / Arts

History, Political Science, Geography, Sociology ya languages mein key terms, timelines/maps, examples aur answer-writing practice karo. Sirf ratta nahi—apne shabdon mein explain karo.

🧠 Level 11 habits challenge

Is hafte: 5 din padhai ka record rakho, 1 weak topic dobara padho, aur Sunday ko 20-minute self-test do.

🎓 Level 12 — Boards & Next Step

Board preparation ko chhote milestones mein baanto. School/board syllabus aur official dates ko priority do.

🗓️ 3-stage plan

Stage 1: syllabus aur weak chapters list karo. Stage 2: chapter-wise questions aur previous/sample papers solve karo. Stage 3: timed mock tests, mistakes notebook aur targeted revision.

✍️ Answer-writing practice

Question ko dhyan se padho, marks ke hisaab se points likho, steps/diagrams jahan zaroori hon dikhाओ, aur aakhir mein answer check karo.

🎯 College / entrance planning

Course ki eligibility, entrance exam, application deadline, fees aur scholarships ko official websites se verify karo. Ek primary option ke saath 2 backup options bhi rakho.

💪 Exam-week wellbeing

Neend, paani, regular meals aur short breaks ka dhyan rakho. Raat bhar jagkar padhne ke bajay repeatable routine rakho.

🏁 Level 12 preparation challenge

Ek sample paper ya 30-minute timed practice karo, phir 3 mistakes likhkar unhe revise karo.
